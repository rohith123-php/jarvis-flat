# jarvisflat
🏛️ System Architecture & Portals
The application is structured into three distinct user portals:

1. 🌐 Public Customer & Discovery Portal (Frontend)
Interactive Property Showcase: Browse residential, commercial, industrial, NRI, and venture listings across key metropolitan cities (Chennai, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, Hyderabad, Dubai, Pune, Delhi).
Property Details & Floor Plans: Detailed view pages showcasing BHK configurations, pricing, photo galleries, amenities, and floor levels.
Instant Inquiry & Schedule Tour: Quick inquiry modal and contact forms for prospective buyers.
Smart Search & Filters: Filter properties by type, city, BHK count, and price range.
2. 👤 Resident & Buyer Portal (/resident)
Self-Service Resident Dashboard: Residents and buyers can monitor their purchased/booked units and payment history.
Cart & Seamless Checkout: Secure booking flow allowing deposit payments, GST calculations, and payment method selection (UPI, Credit Card, Net Banking).
Automated Invoice & Receipts: Instant generation and viewing of printable tax invoices (INV-YYYY-XXXX).
Maintenance & Complaints Helpdesk: Submit tickets for electrical, plumbing, or facility issues and track resolution status in real-time.
Profile Management: Manage contact details and resident information.
3. 🛡️ Enterprise Admin Control Hub (/admin)
Real-time Analytics Dashboard: High-level overview of total inventory, occupancy rates, revenue, pending bookings, and active complaints.
Property & Flat Management: Add, edit, remove, and update inventory, pricing, images, and availability status (Available vs Booked).
Booking & Resident Management: Review customer bookings, approve/reject allocations, and assign units.
Financial & Invoice Hub: Automated tax calculations, payment status reconciliation, and downloadable receipts.
Complaints & Maintenance Operations: Assign tickets, update status (Pending, In Progress, Resolved), and add admin notes.
Notice Board Broadcasting: Publish community announcements and updates.
Audit & Activity Logs: Track admin actions for security and compliance.
Global Customizer & Settings Hub: Dynamic white-labeling system to configure brand name, slogans, theme colors, typography fonts, currency, utility rates, and download one-click SQL database backups.
